Frequently asked questions

What's included in the free assessment?

A 15-minute call where we understand what you need and tell you what makes sense, how long it takes and roughly what it costs. No commitment.

How much does a project cost?

The Pricing section has reference prices. The final price depends on the scope, and we send it to you in writing, free and fixed.

How long does it take?

It depends on the scope: a website takes less than a store or a system. The exact timeline is written into the proposal.

What do I need to get started?

A clear idea of the problem you want to solve. A logo, copy or photos help; if you don't have them, we help you prepare them.

Do you work with clients abroad?

Yes. We work remotely, in English or Spanish.

What happens after launch?

You can manage the system yourself, or hire monthly maintenance and we take care of it.
